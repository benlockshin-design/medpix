DermPix helps clinicians take and organize clinical photos without sending anything to an outside server.

What DermPix does

  • Take photos in the app. Capture clinical images with the iPhone camera, or import photos you already have.
  • Organize by record. Keep related photos in linked records, tagged by condition and treatment.
  • Locked and encrypted. DermPix opens with Face ID or your passcode, and the data it stores is encrypted.
  • Where each photo was taken. If you allow location, DermPix records where each photo was taken and shows it on the photo's info sheet.
  • No accounts, no tracking. DermPix has no server, no ads, and no analytics. Photos leave the app only if you save them to your Photos library.

DermPix is a photo documentation tool. It does not diagnose conditions or replace clinical judgment. Clinicians are responsible for patient consent and for following the privacy rules that apply to their practice.
