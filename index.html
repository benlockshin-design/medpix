Clinical photos, kept on your iPhone.

Medpix helps clinicians take, organize, and share clinical photos without sending anything to an outside server.

Coming soon to the App Store

What Medpix does

  • Take photos in the app. Capture clinical images with the iPhone camera, or import photos you already have.
  • Keep them organized. Photos and notes are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Share when you choose. Export or send photos through email, AirDrop, or another app only when you tap to share.
  • Reminders. Optional reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • No accounts, no tracking. Medpix has no server, no ads, and no analytics.

Medpix is a photo documentation tool. It does not diagnose conditions or replace clinical judgment. Clinicians are responsible for patient consent and for following the privacy rules that apply to their practice.
