Medpix Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Medpix is published by Lockshin Training, LLC ("we" or "us"). The short version: your photos and records stay on your iPhone. We have no server and no accounts, and we never see your photos. Medpix has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Information that stays on your iPhone

Photos you take or import, the records you link them to, the conditions and treatments you tag, and each photo's location (if you allow it) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Medpix encrypts this data. If you back up your iPhone, this data may be included in that backup, depending on your Apple account settings.

Face ID and passcode

Medpix is locked with Face ID or your iPhone passcode. Apple handles this check on your device. Medpix never receives your face data or your passcode.

Camera and Photos

Medpix uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo. A photo from your library is copied into the app only when you select it. Medpix saves a photo to your Photos library only when you choose Save to Photos. Photos in your library may sync to iCloud Photos under your own settings.

Location

If you allow it, Medpix records where each photo was taken and shows it on that photo's info sheet. This location is stored with the photo on your iPhone and is not sent to us. When you open the info sheet, the map is loaded from Apple Maps, so Apple receives the map area being shown, under Apple's own privacy policy. You can turn off location access in Settings, and the rest of the app still works.

Patient information

Medpix is a tool for clinicians. We do not receive, store, or have access to any patient information you capture. You are responsible for getting appropriate patient consent and for handling images in line with the privacy laws and policies that apply to you, including HIPAA where relevant. Medpix is not connected to any medical record system and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

Children

Medpix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your information

You can delete photos in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Photos you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them from Photos.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics, or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date above and post the new version at this address.
