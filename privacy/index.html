Medpix Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Medpix is published by Lockshin Training, LLC ("we" or "us"). The short version: your photos and notes stay on your iPhone. We have no server and no accounts, and we never see your photos. Medpix has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Information that stays on your iPhone

Photos you take or import, notes you add, and your reminder settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. If you back up your iPhone, this data may be included in that backup, depending on your Apple account settings.

Camera and Photos

Medpix uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo. A photo from your library is copied into the app only when you select it. Medpix saves a photo to your Photos library only when you choose to save or export it. Photos in your library may sync to iCloud Photos under your own settings.

Location

If you allow it, Medpix uses your location on your iPhone for in-app features. Your location is not sent to us. You can turn off location access in Settings, and the rest of the app still works.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

Sharing

When you share or export a photo, it goes through the app or service you pick, such as Mail, Messages, or AirDrop, and only when you confirm. We never receive it. Once shared, the privacy practices of that app or service apply. Regular email is not always secure.

Patient information

Medpix is a tool for clinicians. We do not receive, store, or have access to any patient information you capture. You are responsible for getting appropriate patient consent and for handling images in line with the privacy laws and policies that apply to you, including HIPAA where relevant. Medpix is not connected to any medical record system and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

Children

Medpix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your information

You can delete photos in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Photos you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them from Photos.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics, or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date above and post the new version at this address.
