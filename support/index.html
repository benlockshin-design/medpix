Common questions

Where are my photos stored?

Inside the app on your iPhone, encrypted. Medpix has no server and never uploads your photos. A photo goes to your Photos library only if you choose Save to Photos.

How do I unlock Medpix?

Medpix opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode. If Face ID isn't working, go to Settings, then Face ID & Passcode, and make sure Medpix is allowed.

How do I move my photos to a new iPhone?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one. Medpix data comes along with the backup. Turn on iCloud Keychain, or make an encrypted backup, so your records can be opened on the new iPhone.

The camera won't open.

Go to Settings, then Medpix, and make sure Camera is turned on.

I can't import or save photos.

To save a photo to your library, go to Settings, then Medpix, then Photos, and allow Add Photos Only. Importing uses Apple's photo picker and needs no permission.

Why doesn't a photo show where it was taken?

Medpix records a photo's location only if you allow location access. Go to Settings, then Medpix, then Location, and turn it on. Photos taken while location was off won't have a location. Photos imported from your library keep any location already stored in them. The map on the info sheet also needs an internet connection to load.

How do I get a photo out of Medpix?

Open the photo and choose Save to Photos. That is the only way photos leave the app.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual photos in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Photos you saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them from Photos.

Should I keep patient photos in my Photos library?

We recommend keeping clinical photos inside Medpix, where they are locked and encrypted. Photos saved to your library may sync to iCloud Photos and appear alongside your personal pictures.
